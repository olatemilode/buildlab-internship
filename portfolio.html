Lessons Learned

A working deployment isn't the finish line — a deployment that can tell you when something's wrong, and recover on its own, is a different level of maturity. Automating the pipeline removed an entire category of human error: no more manually rebuilding, tagging, or updating services by hand.

Just as valuable was the debugging itself. DNS failures, IAM permission gaps, malformed YAML, Docker auth issues — nearly every real problem came down to reading logs carefully and isolating one variable at a time, not assuming the first error message told the whole story.

With more time: add a load balancer for a stable endpoint, scope IAM permissions to least-privilege, introduce a staging environment, and add automated rollback on failed health checks.
